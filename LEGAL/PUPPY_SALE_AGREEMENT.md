## PREAMBLE

**We care what happens to this dog for the whole of its life.**

The Breeder has invested years in the selection, health testing and development of the
bloodlines from which this dog comes. The Breeder places dogs; the Breeder does not
merely sell them. Our interest in this dog does not end when it leaves us, and it does
not end when the money clears.

The terms that follow are firm, and some of them are unusual. They exist for one reason:
so that no dog we have bred is ever neglected, passed from home to home, or surrendered
to a shelter. If you ever cannot keep this dog — for any reason, without judgement and
without cost to you — it comes home to us. That undertaking runs for the dog's lifetime
and it runs both ways.

This Agreement records the terms on which the dog named above passes into your care, and
the standard of care we ask in return. It takes effect on receipt of payment and
continues for the lifetime of the dog.

☐ **I understand that the Breeder retains a lifelong interest in this dog's welfare, and will always take the dog back.**

## 2. HEALTH AT HANDOVER

2.1 The dog is delivered having received age-appropriate vaccination, deworming and a
veterinary examination. Records are provided with the dog and are available in the
Buyer's client portal.

2.2 The Buyer is advised to have the dog examined by a veterinarian of the Buyer's own
choosing **within seventy-two (72) hours** of delivery. Where such an examination
discloses a pre-existing congenital defect materially affecting the dog's health, the
Buyer must notify the Breeder in writing within seven (7) days, supported by the
veterinarian's written findings.

2.3 The Breeder's obligations in respect of such a defect are set out in clause 3. This
clause does not limit any right the Buyer has under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of
2008.

☐ **I understand the 72-hour examination and 7-day notification periods.**

## 3. HEALTH UNDERTAKING

3.1 The Breeder health-tests breeding stock for dilated cardiomyopathy (DCM1–DCM5), hip
dysplasia and elbow dysplasia. Results for this dog's sire and dam are available to the
Buyer.

3.2 Where a veterinary specialist confirms a **hereditary** defect that materially
affects the dog's quality of life and which manifests before the dog's second birthday,
and the Buyer has complied with clause 2.2 and clause 4, the Breeder will at the
Breeder's election either replace the dog from a future litter or refund a portion of
the purchase price to be agreed.

3.3 The Breeder does not undertake in respect of: conditions arising from injury,
neglect, inadequate nutrition, over-exercise during growth, obesity, failure to
vaccinate, failure to treat parasites, or any condition caused or aggravated by the
Buyer's own conduct.

3.4 Nothing in this clause limits the Buyer's rights under sections 55 and 56 of the
Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008.

☐ **I understand what is and is not covered by the health undertaking.**

## 4. THE BUYER'S OBLIGATIONS OF CARE

The Buyer undertakes, for the lifetime of the dog, to:

4.1 provide adequate, appropriate and sufficient food and clean water at all times;

4.2 provide **shelter that is dry, warm and secure**. The Buyer acknowledges that the
Dobermann is a short-coated breed with limited tolerance for cold and is not suited to
being permanently housed outdoors without adequate warm shelter;

4.3 keep the dog within a securely fenced property and under proper control in public;

4.4 provide veterinary care when required, and maintain vaccination and parasite control;

4.5 provide daily exercise, mental stimulation and human company appropriate to the
breed, and **not** keep the dog in prolonged isolation, permanently chained, or
permanently confined to a cage, run or kennel;

4.6 maintain the dog's microchip registration with the Buyer's current contact details;

4.7 notify the Breeder in writing within fourteen (14) days of any change of the Buyer's
address, telephone number or email address; and

4.8 never surrender, sell, give away, abandon, rehome or dispose of the dog other than in
accordance with clause 6, and never surrender the dog to a shelter, pound, rescue
organisation, laboratory, pet shop, dealer or any similar institution.

☐ **I accept these obligations of care for the dog's lifetime.**
☐ **I understand this breed requires warm, dry shelter and human company, and I will provide both.**

## 5. WELFARE INSPECTION

5.1 The Buyer **consents** to the Breeder, or a person nominated by the Breeder in
writing, inspecting the dog and the premises at which it is kept, in order to satisfy the
Breeder that the obligations in clause 4 are being met.

5.2 Such an inspection will be arranged on reasonable prior notice of not less than
forty-eight (48) hours, at a reasonable time, and will not be conducted more than twice
in any twelve-month period unless the Breeder has reasonable cause for concern.

5.3 Where an in-person inspection is impractical by reason of distance, the Buyer will on
request provide clear, current photographs or video of the dog and its living conditions
within seven (7) days.

5.4 **Unreasonable refusal** to permit an inspection under this clause, or failure to
provide photographs under clause 5.3, is a material breach of this Agreement and entitles
the Breeder to exercise the rights in clause 7.

☐ **I consent to welfare inspection on notice, and to providing photographs where distance makes a visit impractical.**

## 6. NO TRANSFER — RETURN TO THE BREEDER

6.1 **This dog is not transferable.** The Buyer may not sell, give, lend, lease, gift,
bequeath or otherwise part with possession of the dog to any other person.

6.2 If at any time and for any reason the Buyer is unable or unwilling to keep the dog,

6.3 The Breeder may, on written application and entirely at the Breeder's discretion,
approve a transfer to a named alternative home. Any such approval must be **in writing**

force or effect.

6.4 Where the dog is returned under clause 6.2, or is removed under clause 7, **full
ownership of the dog reverts to the Breeder** on the dog coming into the Breeder's
possession, and the Buyer will sign any document reasonably required to record that
reversion.

6.5 The Buyer acknowledges that the purpose of this clause is to ensure that no dog bred
by the Breeder is abandoned, passed between homes, or surrendered to a shelter, and that
the Breeder accepts a lifetime obligation to take back any dog it has bred.

## 8. NO BREEDING RIGHTS

8.1 **This dog is sold with no breeding rights whatsoever.** The dog is placed as a
companion, family or working dog only.

8.2 The Buyer may not mate, breed from, stud out, or permit the mating of the dog,
whether for payment or otherwise, and whether or not a litter results.

8.3 The Buyer may not collect, store, sell or use semen or ova from the dog, and may not
permit the dog to be used in any artificial insemination or assisted reproduction.

8.4 The Buyer may not apply for full or breeding registration of the dog with any kennel
union or registry, nor register any progeny of the dog.

8.5 The Buyer acknowledges that the Breeder's bloodlines are the product of many years of
selection, health testing and expense, and that unauthorised breeding causes the Breeder
real and substantial loss — including loss of stud and puppy income, dilution of the
line, and damage to the Breeder's reputation where dogs of unverified health status
circulate under the Breeder's bloodlines.

8.6 In the event of a breach of this clause, the Buyer will be liable to the Breeder for
**a penalty of `{{breeding_penalty}}` per litter conceived or born in breach**.

8.7 The parties record that this figure is a **genuine pre-estimate of the Breeder's
loss**, arrived at as follows: a Dobermann litter from these bloodlines commonly numbers
between six and ten puppies; the Breeder's own placement prices for such puppies range
from R20 000,00 for a standard puppy to R60 000,00 for an elite developed puppy. The
agreed penalty is materially **below** the gross value of a single elite litter, and does
not attempt to compensate the Breeder for dilution of the bloodline or for reputational
harm caused by dogs of unverified health status circulating under the Breeder's name. The
Buyer confirms that this basis was explained and that the figure is accepted as
reasonable.

8.8 This penalty is payable in addition to, and not in substitution for, the Breeder's
right to recall the dog under clause 7 and to claim any further proven loss.

☐ **I understand that if I breed from this dog I will be liable for a penalty per litter, and the dog may be recalled.**
