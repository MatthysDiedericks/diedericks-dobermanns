## A3. THE BUYER'S OBLIGATION TO CONTINUE DEVELOPMENT

A3.1 The Buyer acknowledges that a developed dog is **partway through its education, not
at the end of it**, and that what has been built will be lost without continued work.

A3.2 The Buyer undertakes to continue this dog's development in **all** of the following
areas, appropriate to its age and stage:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A3.2.1 **Obedience** — maintaining and advancing the foundation laid;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A3.2.2 **Home obedience and manners** — applying that obedience in daily life;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A3.2.3 **Socialisation** — continued positive exposure to people, dogs and situations;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A3.2.4 **Environmental exposure** — continued exposure to novel surfaces, sounds, places and conditions; and

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A3.2.5 **Physical development** — appropriate exercise and conditioning for a growing dog, avoiding over-exercise during growth.

A3.3 Where the Buyer intends this dog to progress in **protection work**, that work must
be undertaken with a **competent, experienced protection trainer**. The Buyer must not
attempt protection training without such a trainer, and must not permit any person to
agitate, provoke or "test" the dog outside structured training with a competent trainer.

A3.4 The Buyer acknowledges that untrained, unqualified or amateur protection work is the
most common cause of ruining a young dog of this type, producing a dog that is unclear,
defensive or dangerous. Doing so is a breach of this Addendum.

A3.5 The Breeder will make reasonable aftercare guidance available to the Buyer, and the
Buyer is encouraged to make use of it.

☐ **I understand that if I want protection work, it must be done with a competent protection trainer.**
