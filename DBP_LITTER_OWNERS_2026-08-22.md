# DogBreederPro — every litter, every puppy, every owner

Captured **22 August 2026** from the DogBreederPro Dogs page, "Litters (155)" preset filter.
That single view lists all litters with each puppy and its buyer — the source we needed for the
121 sold dogs with no owner recorded.

**How to get here again:** DogBreederPro → Dogs → left sidebar → **Litters (155)**.
Do *not* use the Litters page in the Kennel sidebar — it never renders, tested repeatedly.

## How to read the puppy lines

DBP concatenates the puppy's name and the buyer's name with no separator, so a line reads
`Puppy 1 (Pink) Josef Kotse` = puppy "Puppy 1 (Pink)", buyer "Josef Kotse".

Where the puppy was later named, the name appears twice — `Hugo Hugo von Diedericks` is the dog
Hugo, owner Hugo von Diedericks. **Some lines are genuinely ambiguous and must not be parsed
automatically** — `Ade Ade Boomer`, `Zeus Zeus Elmes`, `Blue de Vries`. Matt confirms those.

`Std` and `Elite` in a line record the programme tier that was sold.

## What this tells us

- **Roughly 60 buyer names are recoverable.** The rest were never captured in DBP either — those are genuinely gone, and no amount of digging will produce them.
- **Programme tier is recorded on the 2025 litters** — Std vs Elite, per puppy. That is worth importing; it is the only historical record of what was sold at which tier.
- **Microchip numbers are on many puppies** and are a far more reliable match key than names.
- **DBP has no weights.** The weight grid for Claire × Santini is set up but completely blank. Our system holds 90 weight records for that litter, so we are ahead, not behind.
- **Go-home was 8 weeks until mid-2025, then 10 weeks.** Worth knowing when quoting collection dates.
